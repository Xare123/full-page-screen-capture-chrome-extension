A FEW GOOD THINGS, COLLECTED.

Make room for
a little wonder.

This week: a road that leads to the coast, a recipe worth sharing, and the small pleasure of paying attention.

01 / SOMEWHERE TO GO

The long way is
sometimes the right way.

On the map, it looks like a detour. In real life, it is a line of cypress trees, a roadside farm stand, and a curve in the road where the whole ocean suddenly appears.

We spent a day on the fictional North Point coast with no reservations and a very short list. Here are the moments we would happily do again.

The first stop

A little bakery, still warm bread, and coffee on a bench facing the water.

The last light

One quiet overlook. Bring a layer and stay until the sky changes color.

02 / SOMETHING TO MAKE

A table for whoever
shows up.

Sunday lunch does not need a special occasion. A pot of beans, a crisp salad, and a loaf of good bread are more than enough to begin.

Our favorite detail: setting out an extra plate, just in case.

THE SIMPLE SUNDAY LIST
White beans & seasonal vegetables For the pot
Crusty bread & good olive oil For the table
Citrus & fresh herbs For the finish
“A good week is made of little
things you decided to notice.”
03 / SOMETHING TO KEEP

Three lines a day.

A place. A detail. A feeling. That is the whole notebook practice. No perfect sentences, no pressure to fill a page — just a few words to help the day stay with you.

This week, our pages held a crooked blue door, the smell of rain on warm pavement, and a conversation with a stranger who knew a better way home.

UNTIL NEXT SUNDAY

Keep the good bits.

Save this dispatch for a slower afternoon. Forward it to someone you would like to take the scenic route with.
